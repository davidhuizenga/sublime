Community rules.

Anonymity should make honest conversation easier, not make harm acceptable.

Keep it respectful

Criticize ideas, not people's right to exist. No threats, harassment, hate speech, or targeted abuse.

Protect privacy

Don't post phone numbers, home addresses, private messages, passwords, or identifying details about yourself or someone else.

No illegal or dangerous content

Don't use the community to threaten people, facilitate crimes, or encourage harm.

Remember that posts are public

Posts may be copied, screenshotted, indexed, or archived. Don't assume a public post can be taken back.

Moderation

This starter version publishes posts immediately and does not include a delete or report dashboard. The owner should check the feed regularly and add moderation controls before promoting it widely.
